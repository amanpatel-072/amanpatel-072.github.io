About the Project

Vulnerable youth, domestic migrant workers, and runaway children frequently fall victim to human trafficking cartels, deceptive job offers, and forced labor. Many victims fail to recognize warning signs or know where to seek help.


This system provides trafficking awareness resources, job recruitment risk checking, emergency contacts, and anonymous reporting facilities.


Objective: Develop a responsive, interactive frontend application.


Expected Functionality: Job recruitment red-flag checker, anonymous tip-off form, emergency helpline directory, and child protection guide.

Child Protection Guide

  • Never trust unknown job offers without verification.
  • Share travel details with trusted family members.
  • Avoid handing over identity documents to strangers.
  • Be cautious of promises of high salaries abroad.
  • Contact local authorities immediately if exploitation is suspected.
  • Teach children how to seek help from trusted adults.
